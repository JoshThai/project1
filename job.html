Cybersecurity Specialist

  1. Position: Senior Cybersecurity Specialist
  2. Type of Job: Full Time
  3. Salary Range: $140000 - $200000
  4. Location:
  5. Department: Security (Information & Communication Technology)

About This Role

The main purpose of the role is to provide technical leadership relating to the cyber security requirements and guiding the implementation of cyber controls. The Cyber Security Lead will be a key input to a range of cyber security projects and be the interface between the delivery of the cyber program, with an aim to increase the cyber security maturity and manage cyber risks across the organisation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Protect computer systems and networks from cyber threats.
  • Identify vulnerabilities and develop strategies to mitigate risks.
  • Monitor systems for unusual activities and respond to security incidents.
  • Implement and maintain security measures like firewalls and antivirus software.
  • Conduct security audits and ensure compliance with cybersecurity standards.
  • Educate employees on best practices to prevent cyberattacks.

Required Qualifications and Experiences

  • A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Relevant certifications like CISSP, CISM, or CEH are often preferred
  • Expertise in areas like cloud security, threat intelligence, or compliance frameworks.
  • 2 - 5 years of experience in cybersecurity or related IT fields.
  • Familiar with ASD Essential 8, NIST, AESCSF, PCI DSS and similar security frameworks.

Benifits

  • Diverse Challenges: The work involves staying ahead of evolving cyber threats, which keeps the role interesting and intellectually stimulating.
  • Access to Cutting-Edge Technology: You'll often get to work with the latest tools and systems, keeping your technical skills sharp.
  • Team Collaboration: Being part of a company's security team means collaborating with IT professionals, developers, and even management to protect assets.
